ABSTRACT: Two new diketopyrrolopyrrole-based π-conju- gated copolymers (PDPP6T and PDPP7T) have been synthesized by Stille coupling polymerization of 3,6-bis(5′-bromo-[2,2′-bithiophen]-5-yl)-2,5-bis(2-octyldodecyl)pyrrolo-[3,4-c]pyrrole-1,4(2H,5H)-dione with α , α ′ -bis- (trimethylstannyl)-bithiophene a n d α , α ′ -bis- (trimethylstannyl)-terthiophene, respectively. The impressive high mobility of 3.94 cm<sup>2</sup> V<sup>−1</sup> s <sup>−1</sup> for the polymer with sextetthiophene (6T) and of 2.82 cm<sup>2</sup> V<sup>−1</sup>s<sup>−1</sup>

for polymer with septetthiophene (7T) is acquired. It is found that the introduction

of longer β-unsubstituted oliogothiophene unit in DPP-based copolymers has a great influence on the molecular weight and solubility of the DPP-oligothiophene copolymers that finally affects the organic thin-film transistor (OTFT) performances, indicating that a suitable number of thiophene group in β-unsubstituted oligothiophene exists for such a kind of copolymer to exhibit the best OTFT performances. This work also reveals the significance in the design of D−A copolymers for OTFTs through regulating the balance between π−π stacking of intermolecular chains and molecular weight as well as solubility of the rigid main chain.

### INTRODUCTION

■

Conjugated polymers for organic thin-film transistors (OTFTs)

have been extensively studied due to their easily modification of structure, solution processability, and cheap device fabrication

such as printing technique.<sup>1−5</sup> In the past of few years, great achievements for polymer-based OTFTs have been acquired through molecular design and device optimization. For example,

some polymers exhibit high mobility over 1.0 cm<sup>2</sup> V<sup>−1</sup> s<sup>−1</sup>,<sup>6−24</sup>

even 10.5 cm<sup>2</sup> V<sup>−1</sup> s<sup>−1</sup>.<sup>25</sup> It is well-documented that most of high hole-mobility polymers are donor−acceptor (D−A) type materials including a strong electron-accepting and an electron-donating unit. Because of strong donor−acceptor interaction of the nearest neighboring intermolecular over- lapping and ordered π−π close packing among the conjugated polymeric chains the hole-mobility are greatly enhanced.<sup>3,6,14</sup> Among these D−A copolymers diketopyrrolopyrrole (DPP)- based copolymers have gained much attention for their

8−12,16−21,23−37

promising application in OTFTs.	The strong electron deficiency and good planarity of DPP unit endow the polymer strong intermolecular π−π interactions of the main

chains.<sup>16,17,23</sup> In addition, introduction of a long π-conjugated donor can reinforce the intermolecular stacking interactions for D−A copolymer, which is a feasible synthetic strategy for obtaining high charge carrier mobility.

Recently, many studies have proven that introducing a longer β-unsubstituted oligothiophene unit as an electron-donating unit to construct DPP-oligothiophene copolymers can extend the D−A conjugation length and reduce the steric hindrance of the bulk side chain groups, which does favor to enhance the mobility with much pronounced p-type behavior. DPP-oliogothiphene copolymers.<sup>23</sup> However, it can be expected that as the number of thiophene is increased in the long β-unsubstituted oligothiophene, the conjugated main-chain of D−A copolymers will become more rigid. Thus, the corresponding copolymer will become poor soluble or even insoluble in organic solvents, which will affect molecular weight, film-forming fabrication, polymeric crystallization and intermolecular ordering capability that finally impairs the OTFT performance. On the other hand, the introduction of more thiophene unit can extend the conjugation length of the main-chain, increase the distance of two bulk side-chain groups in DPP unit and diminish their steric hindrance, which favors to form well-ordered lamellar structure of π−π stacking and improve the hole-mobility of the OTFT performances. Therefore, addition of thiophene group in the conjugated backbone may affect the π−π stacking, solubility and film-forming property of polymers, thus influencing the OTFT performances.

Herein, we designed and synthesized two new DPP-oliogothiphene copolymers, diketopyrrolopyrrole-sextetthio- phene (PDPP6T) and diketopyrrolopyrrole-septetthiophene (PDPP7T) copolymers. The remarkably high mobility of 3.94 cm<sup>2</sup> V<sup>−1</sup> s<sup>−1</sup> for PDPP6T and 2.82 cm<sup>2</sup> V<sup>−1</sup> s<sup>−1</sup> for PDPP7T is acquired, and the influence on the OTFT performances through introducing different long β-unsubsituted oligothiophene unit has been investigated.

Finally, the high-molecular-weight samples were obtained by Soxhlet extraction with chlorobenzene (namely PDPP6T-cb and PDPP7T-cb) and dichlorobenzene (namely PDPP6T-dcb and PDPP7T-dcb). The molecular weight and polydispersity index (PDI) of these polymers were determined by gel permeation chromatography (GPC) analysis with a polystyrene standard calibration. M<sub>n</sub> and PDI of these polymers were measured at 150 °C using 1,2,4-trichlorobenzene as an eluent (see Figure S1 in the Supporting Information) and summarized in Table 1. As can be seen, the molecular weight of PDPP6T-cb (M<sub>n</sub> = 19 700, extracted with chlorobenzene) is much less than that of PDPP6T-dcb (M<sub>n</sub> = 40200, extracted with dichlorobenzene). Similar results were also obtained for the PDPP7T samples (PDPP7T-cb, M<sub>n</sub> =15 000; PDPP7T-dcb, M<sub>n</sub> = 25 400). These results are well in agreement with their solubility. The samples extracted with chlorobenzene (PDPPnT- cb, n = 6, 7) can be soluble in hot chloroform and chlorinated benzenes. However, PDPP6T-dcb and PDPP7T-dcb, which was extracted with dichlorobenzene, can be only partially soluble in hot dichlorobenzene and trichlorobenzene. It should be noted that the PDPP7T samples show lower molecular weight than the corresponding PDPP6T samples, which can be attributed to the increment of one more thiophene group in β-unsubsituted oligothiophene unit strengthening the rigidity of D−A unit of the polymeric main-chain and results in the decrease of molecular weight and solubility of the polymer.

The thermogravimetric analysis (TGA) study for these polymers (see Figure S2 in the Supporting Information) show that the decomposition temperatures are over 340 °C, implying that these polymers exhibit much excellent thermostability. The differential scanning calorimetry (DSC) of polymers was also characterized as shown in Figure S3 in the Supporting Information. Thermal analysis of PDPP6T-cb, PDPP7T-cb and PDPP7T-dcb show an endothermic peak at about 248, 294, and 278 °C respectively in the DSC profiles, which refers to the melting of the polymeric backbone. The melting of the side chains was not observed, which means that the crystallization of the polymeric main-chains is predominant during the molecular packing as compared to the long side chains. Unexpectedly, no obvious microstructural phase transitions in the DSC profiles of PDPP6T-dcb were observed below 350 °C. These results indicate that the molecular weight has great effect on the microstructural phase transition of the polymer.

The UV−vis−NIR absorption spectra of these polymers were measured in hot o-dichlorobenzene heated at about 100 °C and in thin film as shown in Figure 1. The corresponding photophysical data are summarized in Table 1. ### CONCLUSIONS

■

Two new D−A copolymers with alternate DPP and β-unsubstituted sextetthiophene (6T) or heptetthiophene (7T) unit were synthesized and characterized. The remarkably high mobility of 3.94 cm<sup>2</sup> V<sup>−1</sup> s <sup>−1</sup> for PDPP6T-cb and of 2.82 cm<sup>2</sup> V<sup>−1</sup> s<sup>−1</sup> for PDPP7T-cb is acquired through annealing at a suitable temperature. The influences of long β-unsubstituted oliogothio- phene unit on the OTFT performances were investigated. By comparison of OTFT mobility of series PDPPnT copolymers, it is found that the introduction of longer β-unsubstituted oligothiophene unit reduces the steric hindrance of the bulk

side-chain groups that favors to construct well-ordered lamellar structure of π−π stacking. However, the strengthening of the intrachain conjugation rigidity results in low molecular weight and poor solubility of the polymer that is unfavorable for OTFT performances. Therefore, there is a suitable number of thiophene groups in β-unsubstituted oligothiophene for PDPPnT copoly- mers to exhibit the highest hole-mobilty and best OTFT performances. The study of influence of the longer β-unsubstituted oliogothiophene unit on the OTFT performances in this work also reveals the significance of regulating the balance between π−π stacking of intermolecular chains and molecular weight as well as solubility of rigid main-chain in the design of D−A copolymers for OTFTs.
