# Self-Assembly and Host-Guest Interaction of Metallomacrocycles Using Fluorescent Dipyrazole Linker with Dimetallic Clips

By employing diimine ligands coordinated dimetallic clips ([(bpy)<sub>2</sub>Pd<sub>2</sub>(NO<sub>3</sub>)<sub>2</sub>\](NO<sub>3</sub>)<sub>2</sub> or [(phen)<sub>2</sub>Pd<sub>2</sub>(NO<sub>3</sub>)<sub>2</sub>\](NO<sub>3</sub>)<sub>2</sub>, where bpy = 2,2<sup>0</sup>-bipyridine, phen = 1,10-phenanthroline) as the corner and anthracene-, naphthalene-, and benzene- based dipyrazolate dianions as the linker, a series of positively charged metallomacrocycles ([M<sub>4</sub>L<sub>2</sub>]<sup>4þ</sup> or [M<sub>8</sub>L<sub>4</sub>]<sup>8þ</sup>) have been synthesized through a directed self-assembly method in aqueous solution. Every macrocycle has a cavity to bind solvent molecules or anions. The structures were characterized by elemental analysis, 1H and 13C NMR, electrospray ionization mass spectrometry, and single crystal X-ray diffraction analysis for compound 1 34PF<sub>6</sub>h (1 = {[(bpy)Pd]<sub>4</sub>L<sup>1</sup><sub>2</sub>}<sup>4þ</sup>), 3 34PF<sub>6</sub>h38CH<sub>3</sub>CN 3H<sub>2</sub>O (3 = {[(bpy)Pd]<sub>4</sub>L<sup>2</sup><sub>2</sub>}<sup>4þ</sup>), and 7 3 4PF<sub>6</sub>h3 6H<sub>2</sub>O (7 = {[(bpy)Pd]<sub>4</sub>L<sup>5</sup><sub>2</sub>}<sup>4þ</sup>). The 1:1 host-guest complexation for anthracene-based dipyrazolate-bridged macrocycles with aromatic guests was investigated via UV-vis and fluorescent titration.

Self-Assembly and Characterization of the [M<sub>4</sub>L<sub>2</sub>]<sup>4þ</sup>-Type Metallomacrocycles. [(bpy)<sub>2</sub>Pd<sub>2</sub>(NO<sub>3</sub>)<sub>2</sub>\](NO<sub>3</sub>)<sub>2</sub> or [(phen)<sub>2</sub>-Pd<sub>2</sub>(NO<sub>3</sub>)<sub>2</sub>\](NO<sub>3</sub>)<sub>2</sub> was treated with a solution containing 0.5 equivalent of H<sub>2</sub>L<sup>n</sup> (n labeled by 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6) (as shown in Scheme 2) in water at room temperature over 2 h, followed by adding acetone into the solution. The resulting mixture was heated at 60 °C for 12 h to produce [M<sub>4</sub>L<sub>2</sub>]- (NO<sub>3</sub>)<sub>4</sub> in quantitative yield with spontaneous deprotona- tion of dipyrazole ligands. The formation of the [M<sub>4</sub>L<sub>2</sub>]<sup>4þ</sup>-type metallomacrocyclic structure is further supported by electrospray ionization mass spectrometry (ESI-MS).
