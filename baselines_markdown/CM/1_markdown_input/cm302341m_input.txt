1-002	Over the past decade, conjugated small organic molecules and main-chain polymers for organic thin-film transistors (OTFTs) have attracted considerable attention due to their easily modification of structure, solution processability, and cheap device fabrication such as printing technique.[1−10] Thanks to the significant efforts of scientists, great achievements for polymer- based OTFTs have been acquired in recent years.
1-003	Some polymer-based OTFTs show high mobility over 1.0 cm2 V−1 s−1.[11−22] It has been found that the introduction of a strong electron-accepting and electron-donating unit into donor− acceptor (D−A) copolymer can greatly enhance the charge transport mobility and efficiently improve the OTFT perform- ances of the devices due to strong donor−acceptor interaction between the nearest neighboring intermolecular overlapping
1-004	and ordered π−π close packing of the polymer chains.[8,11,19]
1-005	However, most of these polymers need thermal annealing at
1-006	high temperature in vacuum or in inert atmosphere to improve
1-007	the molecular ordered-organization of the polymeric main chain in the thin films.
1-008	Obviously such inefficient fabricating processes are unfavorable for the large-scale production of OTFT devices at low cost.
1-009	Therefore, to meet the demand of

1-011	the industrial application, design and synthesis of high mobility OTFT polymers without annealing or annealed directly in an air atmosphere is a great challenge.

1-014	application in OTFTs.
1-015	The strong electron- deficiency of DPP unit endows the D−A copolymer ambipolar (hole and electron) transport properties and the strong

1-017	intermolecular interaction.
1-018	For example, some diketopyyrolopyrrole-oligothiophene copolymer exhibited the excellent hole-transport property with high hole mobility annealing at suitable temperature (PDPP3T, μ = 1.73 cm2 V−1 s −1;[34] PDPP4T, μ = 0.9714 or 1.0417 cm 2 V−1 s −1).

1-025	excessive electron-accepting effect of DPP, but it also slightly weakens the main-chain interaction, which is favorable to enhance the molecular ordering capability of the polymer at lower temperature and efficiently improve the hole mobility of the OTFTs.
1-026	Thus, it exhibited high mobility of 0.89 cm2 V−1 s−1 even without thermal annealing for PDPP4T.[14,17]
1-027	In order to further enhance the mobility of the OTFTs and also to better understand the correlation of the molecular structure and OTFT performance, we synthesize a new conjugated main-chain copolymer, diketopyrrolopyrrole-quin- tetthiophene copolymer (PDPP5T) with much high molecular weight and low polydispersity, which contains a DPP group and a β-unsubstituted quintetthiophene (5T) in the repeating unit.
1-028	We expect that the introduction of more thiophene group into diketopyrrolopyrrole-oligothiophene copolymer can extend the conjugation length of donor blocks and make the p-type behavior of the polymer more pronounced that will lead to the enhancement of the hole mobility of the OTFT device.
1-029	On the other hand, introducing the 5T unit into DPP-based copolymer can increase the distance of two bulk side-chain groups in the DPP unit and diminish their intermolecular steric hindrance, which is favorable for π−π stacking to form a well-ordered lamellar structure and enhance the OTFT mobility.
1-030	As expected, the OTFT performance of the PDPP5T-based devices is greatly improved.
1-031	The hole mobility reaches 1.08 cm2 V−1 s−1 without thermal annealing, and a much higher hole mobility of 3.46 cm2 V−1 s−1 is obtained annealed at 200 °C even in an air atmosphere.
1-032	Here we present the synthesis, photophysical and electrochemical properties, and the OTFT performances of PDPP5T.

2-005	Synthesis.
2-006	Synthesis of PDPP5T. Compound 1 (158 mg, 0.154 mmol), compound 2 (88.8 mg, 0.154 mmol), and Pd(PPh3)4 (9 mg, 5 mol %) were added into a flask.
2-007	Then it was subjected to five successive cycle of vacuum followed by refilling with argon, and 10 mL of anhydrous toluene was added via a syringe.
2-008	The polymerization was carried out at 90 °C for 60 h under the protection of argon atmosphere.
2-009	Afterward, 0.5 mL of bromobenzene was added to the mixture to react with the residual trimethylstannyl end group.
2-010	The mixture was kept stirring at 90 °C for 8 h.
2-011	Then it was cooled to ambient temperature and dropped into methanol.
2-012	The raw product was filtered off and subjected to subsequently Soxhlet extraction with acetone, methanol, hexane, chloroform, and finally chlorobenzene.
2-013	The chlorobenzene fraction was collected, concentrated, and precipitated from methanol.
2-014	The product was collected as dark green solid in a yield of 52%.

2-019	The raw product was filtered off and subsequently subjected to Soxhlet extractions with acetone, methanol, and hexane to remove the impurities and oligomers.
2-020	Finally, the polymer was obtained by Soxhlet extraction with chloroform.

2-036	Fabrication and Characterization of Thin-Film Transistors.
2-037	Bottom-gate and top-contact TFT test devices with PDPP5T and PDDP5T-Low as the semiconductor were prepared in silicon wafer in ambient conditions without special precautions.
2-038	The device was fabricated on n+-doped silicon wafer with a 300 nm thermal silicon dioxide.
2-039	The n+-Si layer was used as the gate electrode, and the silicon oxide layer served as the gate dielectric.
2-040	The dielectrics have been modified by octadecyltrichloro-silane (OTS).

4-001	An alternating copolymer PDPP5T containing DPP and a quintetthiophene unit has been synthesized and characterized.
4-002	The introduction of more β-unsubstituted thiophene groups into the main-chain of PDPP5T polymer not only makes the p- type behavior of the polymer more pronounced but also slightly reduces the steric hindrance of the bulk side-chain groups in the DPP unit that favors to construct a well-ordered lamellar structure of π−π stacking after solution deposition and to strengthen the molecular ordering capability of the polymer at lower temperature, so it exhibits a very high hole-mobility of 1.08 cm2 V−1 s−1 without thermal annealing.
4-003	In addition, the annealing process at high temperature reduces the roughness of the thin film and increases the crystallinity of PDPP5T chains.
4-004	Thus, its hole-mobility reaches 3.46 cm2 V −1 s−1 annealed directly in an air atmosphere, which has been confirmed by the XRD and GIXRD patterns and AFM images.
4-005	In brief, PDPP5T is an encouraging OTFT material due to its excellent physical properties and excellent device perform- ances.
4-006	Easy synthesis and high mobility annealed directly in air atmosphere or even without annealing makes it become a promising material for the cost-effective production of OTFTs in a high-throughput and roll-to-roll manufacturing manner.
4-007	Further structural modification for PDPPNT copolymer and device optimization for the OTFT performance is very promising and studying is still under way.
