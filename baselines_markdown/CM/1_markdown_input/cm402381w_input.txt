1-001	■
1-002	Conjugated polymers for organic thin-film transistors (OTFTs)
1-003	have been extensively studied due to their easily modification of structure, solution processability, and cheap device fabrication
1-004	such as printing technique.[1−5] In the past of few years, great achievements for polymer-based OTFTs have been acquired through molecular design and device optimization.
1-005	For example,
1-006	some polymers exhibit high mobility over 1.0 cm2 V−1 s−1,[6−24]
1-007	even 10.5 cm2 V−1 s−1.[25]
1-008	It is well-documented that most of high hole-mobility polymers are donor−acceptor (D−A) type materials including a strong electron-accepting and an electron-donating unit.
1-009	Because of strong donor−acceptor interaction of the nearest neighboring intermolecular over- lapping and ordered π−π close packing among the conjugated polymeric chains the hole-mobility are greatly enhanced.[3,6,14]
1-010	Among these D−A copolymers diketopyrrolopyrrole (DPP)- based copolymers have gained much attention for their
1-011	8−12,16−21,23−37
1-012	promising application in OTFTs.
1-013	The strong electron deficiency and good planarity of DPP unit endow the polymer strong intermolecular π−π interactions of the main
1-014	© 2013 American Chemical Society 4290
1-015	chains.[16,17,23]
1-016	In addition, introduction of a long π-conjugated donor can reinforce the intermolecular stacking interactions for D−A copolymer, which is a feasible synthetic strategy for obtaining high charge carrier mobility.
1-017	Recently, many studies have proven that introducing a longer β-unsubstituted oligothiophene unit as an electron-donating unit to construct DPP-oligothiophene copolymers can extend the D−A conjugation length and reduce the steric hindrance of the bulk side chain groups, which does favor to enhance the mobility with much pronounced p-type behavior.
1-018	For example, several groups have reported that the DPP-oliogothiphene copolymers (PDPPnT, n = 2, 3, 4) show the enhancing hole-mobility with
1-019	9,12,17,27,35,36
1-020	the increment of thiophene group.
1-021	Our group also reported that PDPP5T exhibited remarkably high mobility of 3.46 cm2 V−1 s−1 due to the introduction of quintetthiphene group, which is one of the highest hole-mobilities among the

1-023	Revised: October 12, 2013
1-024	Published: October 15, 2013
1-025	DPP-oliogothiphene copolymers.[23]
1-026	However, it can be expected that as the number of thiophene is increased in the long β-unsubstituted oligothiophene, the conjugated main-chain of D−A copolymers will become more rigid.
1-027	Thus, the corresponding copolymer will become poor soluble or even insoluble in organic solvents, which will affect molecular weight, film-forming fabrication, polymeric crystallization and intermolecular ordering capability that finally impairs the OTFT performance.
1-028	On the other hand, the introduction of more thiophene unit can extend the conjugation length of the main-chain, increase the distance of two bulk side-chain groups in DPP unit and diminish their steric hindrance, which favors to form well-ordered lamellar structure of π−π stacking and improve the hole-mobility of the OTFT performances.
1-029	Therefore, addition of thiophene group in the conjugated backbone may affect the π−π stacking, solubility and film-forming property of polymers, thus influencing the OTFT performances.
1-030	Herein, we designed and synthesized two new DPP-oliogothiphene copolymers, diketopyrrolopyrrole-sextetthio- phene (PDPP6T) and diketopyrrolopyrrole-septetthiophene (PDPP7T) copolymers.
1-031	The remarkably high mobility of 3.94 cm2 V−1 s−1 for PDPP6T and 2.82 cm2 V−1 s−1 for PDPP7T is acquired, and the influence on the OTFT performances through introducing different long β-unsubsituted oligothiophene unit has been investigated.

3-080	Thus, the Mn of PDPP7T-cb (15.0k) is lower than that of PDPP6T-cb (19.7k).
3-081	For PDPP6T-dcb and PDPP7T-dcb, they can be only partially soluble in hot dichlorobenzene and trichlorobenzene, indicating that their solubility becomes much poorer than PDPP6T-cb and PDPP7T-cb.
3-082	Although they show much higher molecular weight than PDPPnT-cb (n = 6, 7), the poor solubility of them is unfavorable for them to form the ordered interchain packing for the charge transport, so they present much worse hole mobility than the corresponding PDPPnT-cb (n = 6, 7), which is in agreement with the AFM images (see Figure S4 in the Supporting Information).
3-083	Therefore, it can be concluded that the suitable β-unsubstituted oligothiophene (i.e., 5T and 6T) for the DPP-oligothiophene copolymers can balance the influence on OTFT performances between the favorable factor for well-ordered π−π stacking of intermolecular chains and the adverse factor for low molecular weight and poor solubility of the rigid main chain, which is important in the design of conjugated polymers for OTFTs.
4-001	■
4-002	Two new D−A copolymers with alternate DPP and β-unsubstituted sextetthiophene (6T) or heptetthiophene (7T) unit were synthesized and characterized.
4-003	The remarkably high mobility of 3.94 cm2 V−1 s −1 for PDPP6T-cb and of 2.82 cm2 V−1 s−1 for PDPP7T-cb is acquired through annealing at a suitable temperature.
4-004	The influences of long β-unsubstituted oliogothio- phene unit on the OTFT performances were investigated.
4-005	By comparison of OTFT mobility of series PDPPnT copolymers, it is found that the introduction of longer β-unsubstituted oligothiophene unit reduces the steric hindrance of the bulk
4-006	side-chain groups that favors to construct well-ordered lamellar structure of π−π stacking.
4-007	However, the strengthening of the intrachain conjugation rigidity results in low molecular weight and poor solubility of the polymer that is unfavorable for OTFT performances.
4-008	Therefore, there is a suitable number of thiophene groups in β-unsubstituted oligothiophene for PDPPnT copoly- mers to exhibit the highest hole-mobilty and best OTFT performances.
4-009	The study of influence of the longer β-unsubstituted oliogothiophene unit on the OTFT performances in this work also reveals the significance of regulating the balance between π−π stacking of intermolecular chains and molecular weight as well as solubility of rigid main-chain in the design of D−A copolymers for OTFTs.
