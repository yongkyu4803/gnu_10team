1-024	versatile solvent choices, and high hole mobility (up to 1.4 cm2 V−1 s−1 without solution shear, and up to 4.6 cm2 V−1 s−1 with optimized solution shear).[6−12] Various approaches, including additives and external field-induced alignment methods have been adopted to manipulate the crystallization of TP, to optimize its charge transport within the resultant crystalline thin films.
1-025	For example, Giri et al. used a solution shear method to apply lattice strain on the TP unit cell, reducing the π−π stacking distance from 3.33 Å to 3.08 Å, and increasing the hole mobility of TP by 5−6 fold.[6]
1-026	Other external alignment methods applied to control the crystallization of TP include substrate tilting,[13] air navigation,[9,14,15] dip-coating,[8] and scanning corona- discharge coating.[16]
1-027	In the additive approaches, nonconjugated polymers (such as poly(α-methylstyrene))[10,17] and insulating SiO2 nanoparticles[18] were used to manipulate the vertical and lateral phase separation in the TP-based blends, while poor solvents were often introduced to yield solution-processed TP single crystals.[11,19]
1-028	Thin films of TP-based blends with conjugated polymer additive poly[bis(4-phenyl)(2,4,6-trimethylphenyl)amine] (PTAA) were previously fabricated with a spin-coating method, yielding spherulitic TP crystals on the top surface of the blend film.[17]
1-029	It is noted that no TP unit cell changes were reported for most of these external alignment and additive approaches, except in the case of the solution shear method.[6]
1-030	Slow solution crystallization of small molecule organic semiconductors with conjugated polymer additives have not been reported previously to the best of our knowledge.
1-031	In addition, structural similarity between the additive and small molecule organic semiconductor are largely underexplored with regard to their effects on resultant crystal structures and long-range order.
1-032	Using TP as a model small molecule semiconductor and two conjugated polymers with distinctively different structural similarity to TP, we demonstrate for the first time, “conjugated polymer-mediated polymorphism” of small molecule organic semiconductor for the first time, which leads to excellent long-range order and improved charge transport in the absence of any external alignment techniques.
