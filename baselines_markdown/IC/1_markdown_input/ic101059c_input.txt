2-050	The enantioselectivity found in all the reactions is very poor com- pared with the values reported by Toste et al. using as catalyst the binuclear system (R)-DTBM-SEGPHOS-gold(I) (Table 1, entry 10),[14] but it has to be considered that phosphine cata- lysts (and also NHC systems) are better understood and have been much more elaborated in the past years.
2-051	In these initial studies of the new HBHC and NAC ligands we see that the best enantioselectivity results, even if modest (11-24% ee), have been obtained with catalysts 4, 5, 9, and 10, all based on the axially dissymmetric 1,10-binaphthyl ligand.
2-052	Obviously further elaboration of these ligands looks more promising than for mononuclear complexes.
2-053	For this reason, we hoped that catalysts 13 and 14, based on the bulkier (3,30-diphenyl)-1,10-binaphthyl chiral system might be more enantioselective than 9 and 10, but surprisingly they are clearly worse.
2-054	Since three conformers are present in solution for 13 and 14, it might happen that they induced opposite enantioselectivity, in detriment of the overall enantiomeric excess.
2-055	Some chiral gold(I) HBHC and NAC complexes were also checked for the enantioselective hydroalkoxylation of the γ-hydroxyallene 17, and the results are gathered in Table 2.
2-056	Again, the best enantioselectivity results (22 and 20% ee, Table 2, entries 3 and 4) are obtained with 4 and 9.
2-057	This result is similar to those obtained using binuclear complexes of the type μ-(P-P*)[AuCl]2 with 2,2-bis(diarylphosphino)-biphenyl ligands such as binap or (3,5-xylyl)binap, but much lower that those obtained using the bulkier (S)-DTMB-MeOBI- PHEP as the chiral ligand (Table 2, entry 6).[15]
3-001	This study confirms the catalytic possibilities of the HBHC and NAC gold systems that we have recently introduced in gold catalysis as alternatives to phosphine and NHC ligands.
3-002	In effect, the results show that the gold(I) complexes with these ligands are compatible with the conditions and reagents involved
3-003	Inorganic Chemistry, Vol. 49, No. 21, 2010 9761
3-004	in the cyclopropanation of vinyl arenes and the intramole- cular hydroalkoxylation of allenes.
3-005	Moreover, it is demon- strated that enantioselectivity can be induced with these two kinds of ligands.
3-006	Certainly the enantiomeric excesses are still poor.
3-007	This is due to the linear coordination of gold,[25] which places the reactive coordination site far away from the ancillary chiral ligand.
3-008	However, this is the same problem encountered initially with phosphine and NHC ligands, which have been continuously improved in the past years after successive structural elaborations.
3-009	Only a few ligand variations have been tested in our initial studies, where the purpose was not still to find optimized conditions for a specific synthesis, but to explore the applicability of these ligand types.
3-010	The fact that introducing structural and electronic variations in the NAC ligands is easier (particularly for the NAC ligands) than in phosphines or NHCs offers many opportunities for a more flexible tuning of these ligand to the demands of specific rea- gents, and holds a promise for improved activity and enan- tioselectivity.
3-011	Here we find that dinuclear gold complexes offer higher enantioselectivity and are, in principle, the struc- tures of choice for further elaboration.
