3-001	Synthesis.
3-002	The ligand 5-(pyrimidyl)tetrazole was syn- thesized in situ by [2 þ 3] cycloaddition of azide to 2-cyanopyrimidine.
3-003	Such type of reaction leading to the formation of tetrazoles is well documented in the lite- rature.[11-18] Metal ions Co(II) or Mn(II) upon reaction with 2-cyanopyrimidine in presence of excess azide formed the complexes 1 and 2, respectively, under hy- drothermal condition.
3-004	Complex 1 was also obtained by treating Co(NO3)2 36H2O with a mixture of 1 equiv of Na-pmtz and excess NaN 3 in water at room temperature.
3-005	However, attempts to prepare these complexes using 1 equiv of azide at both room temperature and high tem- perature were unsuccessful.
3-006	Hence, excess azide played an important role in driving the reaction toward the forward direction.
3-007	The purity of both complexes was confirmed from their powder XRD diffraction patterns which matched well with the calculated patterns obtained from their single crystal X-ray structures (Figure 1).
3-008	The peaks in the IR spectra of 1 and 2 at 1571, and 1578 cm-1(s) are due to the tetrazole moiety, and they also showed very intense peaks corresponding to azido at 2122 and 2120 cm-1, respectively.

3-087	Ferroelectric Study of Complex 1.
3-088	Ferroelectric crystals which exhibit a spontaneous electric polarization arising from polar displacements of anions and cations are drawing significant attentions because of their potential applications in the field of telecommunication, ultrasonic devices, electro-optical devices, and switchable NLO (nonlinear optical) devices.
3-089	Initially ferroelectric beha- vior was noticed in pure inorganic complexes like KDP, BaTiO3 but because of various applications of this type of ferroelectric materials, several new kinds of ferroelectric materials have been reported.
3-090	A literature survey reveals that several metal complexes like Cd(II) imidazole,[38] Ag(I) imidazole,[39] Cu(II) with substituted pyrazole,[40]
3-091	Co(II) piperazine complex,[41] and diethyldithiocarbamate complex of Cu(II)[42-44] exhibit ferroelectric behavior.
3-092	In addition to transition metal complexes, several lantha- nides complexes (Sm, Gd, Eu) have also been found to be potentially ferroelectric.[45]
3-093	A new addition to this ferro- electric material is the template borate of Ni(II) complex.[46]
3-094	A ferroelectric ferromagnet which is one of the multi- ferroics has received significant attention because of this multifunctionality.
3-095	As both ferroelectric and ferromagnetic properties are mutually exclusive, systems with multiferroic behavior are very rare.
3-096	Among them ethyl lactate complex with Tb, hexacyanoferrate with rubidium, manganese, and dimethylammonium metal formates are some examples which show multiferroic behavior.[26]
3-097	Complex 1 in the present study crystallized in a non-centrosymmetric space group Aba2 which is associated with the point group C2v that is one of the 10 polar point groups (C1, Cs, C2, C2v, C4, C4v, C3, C3v, C6, C6v) required for ferroelectric behavior.
3-098	To our knowledge until now no multiferroic metal-tetrazole complex has been reported.
3-099	In this respect complex 1 is a novel example which reveals both magnetism and ferroelectricity.
3-100	The ferroelectric data of the powder pellet sample of 1 were measured at room temperature at different voltages.
3-101	Polarization (P) versus applied electric field (E) plots at room temperature with applying field up to (30 KV cm-1 showed (Figure 9) a clear electric hysteresis loop.
3-102	The remnant electric polari- zation (Pr) and coercive field (Ec) were calculated to be 0.08 μC cm-2 and of 4.73 KV cm-1, respectively.
3-103	The satu- ration spontaneous polarization (Ps) of 0.29 μC cm-2 is little higher than that of a typical ferroelectric compound NaKC4H4O6 34H2O (Ps = 0.25 μC cm-2).
3-104	Interestingly, even at 100 K electric hystersis loop was clearly no- ticed at 1500 V with calculated Pr and Ec values of 0.09 μC cm-2 and 5.14 KV cm-1, respectively, having spontaneous polarization Ps = 0.28 μC cm-2 (Figure 9).
