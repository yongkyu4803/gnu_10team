1-001	■
1-002	Benzothiazoles are sulfur- and nitrogen-containing heterocycles that have emerged in their usage as a core structure for diversified therapeutic applications, including antimicrobial and antifungal,[1,2] cytotoxic,[3] and antidiabetic[4] uses.
1-003	In recent years, an increasing number of 2-substituted benzothiazoles have been designed and synthesized for biological evaluations.
1-004	These het- erocycles include the immunosuppressive drug frentizole (I), the antiparasitic tioxidazole (II), a cathepsin-D inhibitior (III), a PPARγ receptor activator (IV), a heat shock protein-90 inhib- itor (V), a human CCR1 and CCR3 receptor antagonist (VI), an antiproliferative (VII), and the calcium channel blocker fostedil (VIII) (Figure 1).[5]
1-005	These moieties are also found in fluorescent pH indicators,[6] an iminocoumarin-based zinc sen- sor,[7] a bioluminogenic substrate,[8] cleavage agents for soluble oligomers of amyloid β-peptides,[9] and ligands for catalytic reac- tions.[10,11]
1-006	Earlier, several methods for the synthesis of benzothiazole derivatives have been reported, including condensation of 2-
1-007	conversion of 2-haloamides into the corresponding thioamides using P4S10 or Lawesson’s reagent, which is generally not feasible for substrates containing ketone, ester, and amide moieties;[20] cyclization of thioformanilides with the aid of transition-metal catalysts[21] under SNAr[22] or radical condi- tions;[23] and cyclization of an arylthioamide using potassium ferricyanide (Jacobson’s method).[24]
1-008	These methods suffer from disadvantages such as harsh reaction conditions, use of toxic metal catalysts and reagents, air-sensitive and toxic substances, cumbersome workup procedures, generation of acidic and me- tallic wastes. etc.
1-009	Recently, Katritzky and co-workers reported a metal-free environmentally benign synthesis of 2-substituted benzothiazoles in water under microwave (MW) irradiation.[25]
1-010	However, this methodology utilizes substrates that are soluble
1-011	in water only, which poses significant challenges.

2-002	In a recent communication, our group reported that 2-O- substituted benzothiazoles could be readily prepared from sugar-based benzotriazolemethanethiones via cyclative cleavage catalyzed by tributyltin hydride (n-Bu3SnH).[27]
2-003	We speculated that a similar transformation would occur to provide 2-(N,N- dialkylamino)benzothiazoles 4 and 2-(aryl/alkylthio)-benzothiazoles 13 if the nucleophiles were replaced with amines and thiols, respectively.
2-004	We undertook an investigation to explore this possibility and synthesized N,N-dialkylthiocar- bamoylbenzotriazoles (R1R2NCSBt, 3) by thiocarbamoylation of secondary amines 1 with bis(benzotriazole)methanethione 2 in a very short time span of 10−15 min (Scheme 1).

2-006	At first sight indeed, N,N-dialkylthiocarbamoylbenzotriazoles 3 have several important features.
2-007	First is their preparation from 2, which in turn can be prepared easily from readily available benzotriazole.
2-008	Second, these compounds should incorporate a relatively weak N−N bond β to the thiocarbonyl moiety, similar to Barton−McCombie deoxygenation,[29] in which a C−O single bond β to a thiocarbonyl moiety is reduced to C−H. Thus, treatment of 1-(2-fluorophenyl)piperazine (1a) with com- pound 2 in the presence of Et3N (0.2 equiv) in anhydrous CH 2Cl2 at room temperature furnished (1H-benzo[d][1,2,3]-triazol-1-yl)(4-(2-fluorophenyl)piperazin-1-yl)methanethione (3a) quantitatively in a very short reaction time.

2-089	Mechanistic Considerations.
2-090	The thiocarbonyl derivatives of primary and secondary alcohols can be reduced easily by n-Bu3 SnH using AIBN as a radical initiator.[40]
2-091	In such reactions, the 2-cyanoprop-2-yl radical generated from AIBN initiates the process by rapidly reacting with tin hydride to produce a tin radical, which attacks the thione function of thiocarbonyl and propagates the reaction.
2-092	A similar mechanism of radical cyclization can also be argued with (TMS) 3SiH, which upon The Journal of Organic Chemistry Article

2-099	The generated intermediate A is further reduced by H· radical followed by subsequent attack of a silyl radical to produce radical intermediate B, which decomposes to give resonance-stabilized nitrogen-centered biradical C via N−N bond cleavage.
2-100	The stabilization of C through delocalization of the electron density over the aryl ring results in the elimination of a nitrogen molecule followed by a consequent intramolecular
2-101	attack of sulfur, producing resonance-stabilized nitrogen-centered radical D, which furnishes benzothiazole 4 via a ther- modynamically favored and thermally induced oxidation pro- cess leading to aromatization at the cost of two σ bonds to form a π bond (Figure 2).
2-102	Several mechanisms could be operative.

2-109	We exploited here the addition of silyl radical onto the thiocarbonyl moiety and investigated the reductive cyclization reported with n-Bu3SnH[41] and subsequent oxidation of the dihydro product.
2-110	The reduc- tive cyclization using (TMS)3SiH possibly includes “oxidation” by either autooxidation or a process in which (TMS)3SiH acts as a base and abstracts the acidic proton from the cyclized dihydro intermediate.[42]
2-111	The oxidation process may be pho- tochemical or thermal, as both are thermodynamically favorable and lead to aromatization at the cost of two σ bonds to form a π bond in the final product.
2-112	However, the problem is explaining how the hydrogen is lost.
2-113	Moreover, it was difficult to observe N2 and H 2 in the stream of nitrogen and under refluxing conditions.
2-114	Additionally, the possibility of auto or air oxidation of the dihydro product was ruled out because the reaction was carried out under inert conditions.
2-115	However, this could be effectively explained by considering (TMS)3SiH as a base, which abstracts hydrogen from the radical intermediate C2 or D2 and affords the benzothiazole 4 as product.
2-116	Hence, the silane is not only catalytic and reductive but also acts as a base.
2-117	The mechanism of the reaction was also supported by den- sity functional theory (DFT), where the geometries of inter- mediate A and compound N,N-dimethyl-1H-benzo[d][1,2,3]-triazole-1-carbothioamide (3g) were optimized at the B3LYP/6-31G(d,p) level in the gas phase using the Gaussian 03package.[43]
2-118	Gaussview 3.09 was used to visualize the optimized molecular geometries, bond lengths, and bond angles.
2-119	The cal- culations at this level provided evidence for a strong interaction between silicon and the sulfur of the thiocarbonyl group in intermediate A (Figure 4).
2-120	In addition, close observation of the IR frequencies of the optimized molecule 3g and intermediate A calculated by DFT predict an enhancement in the intensity of the N1−N2 stretching frequency, while the interaction of 3g with (TMS)3Si· radical suggests the cleavage of benzotriazole ring (for details, see Figures S9−S12 and Tables S9−S12 in the Supporting Information).
3-001	We have developed a concise and efficacious benzotriazole- mediated, two-step, novel and toxic-metal-free approach that provides easy access to a diverse range of 2-N/S/C-substituted benzothiazoles under mild conditions.
3-002	The developed method- ology also demonstrates significant compatibility under micro- wave conditions and is important as it avoids the use of toxic
3-003	n-Bu3 SnH for radical cyclization.
3-004	The synthesis of 2-(N,N- dialkylamino)benzothiazoles, 2-(aryl/alkyl)benzothiazoles, and 2-(aryl/alkylthio)benzothiazoles via benzotriazole ring cleavage using (TMS) 3SiH as radical reducing agent had not been realized previously, and thus, this approach should be of further interest to synthetic and medicinal chemists.
3-005	The short reaction period, simple workup, high yield, and mild conditions of this methodology express significant tolerance toward a number of functional groups such benzyl ethers, acetals, thioethers, esters, aryl halides (Ar−F and Ar−Cl), and alkenes.
3-006	In addition to the generality with respect to the substrate scope, the facile access to the starting materials is also highly appealing.
3-007	The developed methodology performs well in both small and gram-scale syntheses of 2-N/S/C-substituted benzothiazoles and thus may have industrial significance.
